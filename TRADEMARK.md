## What you MAY do (no permission needed)

- **Refer to Phelix by name, factually.** You can say your project "works with
  Phelix", "is compatible with Phelix", "is based on Phelix", or "is a fork of
  Phelix", as long as the statement is true and does not suggest we produce,
  endorse, or support your version. This is normal nominative use.
- **Keep our copyright and license notices** in the source, as the license
  requires. Leaving the `Copyright 2026 Mohammad Abdorrahmani` notice in place is expected and
  correct — that is attribution, not a trademark grant.
- **Use the name in documentation, articles, tutorials, talks, and comparisons.**
- **Discuss, review, or criticize** Phelix.

## What you MAY NOT do (without our written permission)

- **Do not name your fork, distribution, product, or service "Phelix"** or any
  name that is confusingly similar (e.g. "Phelix Pro", "Phelix Cloud",
  "OpenPhelix", "Phelyx", "Felix Deploy"). If you distribute a modified version,
  **give it your own distinct name.**
- **Do not use the Phelix logo or the dashboard's visual identity** for your own
  product, service, site, or app.
- **Do not imply endorsement, affiliation, or partnership** with Mohammad Abdorrahmani, or
  present your build as the official Phelix.
- **Do not use "Phelix" (or confusingly similar terms) in** company names,
  product names, domain names, app-store listings, social media handles, or
  published package names (npm, crates.io, Docker Hub, Homebrew, Go module
  paths, etc.).
- **Do not use our marks in advertising, SEO, or paid keywords** in a way that
  diverts users looking for the official Phelix to a competing offering.

## Forks and redistribution — the short version

You are free (under the license) to fork the code and to contribute changes
back. If you **publish or distribute** your own build, you must:

1. Rename it to something clearly distinct from "Phelix".
2. Remove the Phelix logo and dashboard branding from your distribution.
3. Keep the `LICENSE` file and copyright notices intact.
4. Not describe your build in a way that implies it is the official Phelix.

Contributions submitted **back** to the official Phelix project (see
[`CONTRIBUTING.md`](./CONTRIBUTING.md)) do not need to be renamed — they become
part of Phelix.

If you have a use that falls outside the above — for example, a genuine

- **Website:** https://phelix.anophel.com

misunderstanding later.
