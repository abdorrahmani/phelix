# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in the Phelix community a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

As contributors, maintainers, and community members, we pledge to create an environment where everyone can participate openly, share ideas, ask questions, provide feedback, and collaborate constructively.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful, considerate, and constructive in all interactions.
- Welcoming newcomers and helping others learn and contribute.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback professionally.
- Respecting different perspectives, experiences, and technical approaches.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes, learning from them, and responding positively to feedback.
- Communicating clearly and respectfully in issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project channels.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or conduct based on personal characteristics or identity.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or sustained hostile behavior.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or sexually explicit language or imagery.
- Insults, humiliation, or deliberately hostile criticism directed at individuals.
- Any other conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional and collaborative community.

Disagreement about technical decisions is normal and encouraged when expressed respectfully. Criticism should focus on ideas, code, and decisions rather than attacking individuals.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all spaces operated by the Phelix project, including:

- The Phelix GitHub repository, issues, pull requests, and discussions.
- Project-related communication channels and community spaces.
- Other situations where an individual is officially representing Phelix or its community.

It also applies when community interactions outside these spaces have a direct and meaningful impact on the safety or well-being of the Phelix community.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct and may take appropriate corrective action in response to unacceptable behavior.

Maintainers will assess reported incidents fairly, consider the context and available evidence, and take action appropriate to the circumstances.

Possible responses include:

1. **Warning:** A private or public warning about the behavior and its impact.
2. **Temporary restriction:** A temporary limitation on participation in project spaces.
3. **Removal of content:** Removal or modification of content that violates this Code of Conduct.
4. **Temporary suspension:** A temporary suspension from participation in project activities.
5. **Permanent ban:** Permanent exclusion from project spaces in serious or repeated cases.

Maintainers may choose the response they consider appropriate based on the severity, frequency, and impact of the behavior. They are not required to follow these steps in order.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please contact the Phelix project maintainers at:

**Email:** [phelix@anophel.com](mailto:phelix@anophel.com)

When reporting an incident, please include as much relevant information as you feel comfortable sharing, such as:

- A description of the incident.
- The date and location of the incident, including relevant GitHub issues, pull requests, or discussions.
- Links, screenshots, or other relevant evidence, if available.
- Any additional context that may help the maintainers understand the situation.

Please do not include unrelated personal information or sensitive data.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly as reasonably possible. Maintainers will respect the privacy of reporters and affected individuals to the extent practical and appropriate for investigating and addressing the incident.

Please use this email for Code of Conduct concerns rather than general technical support or feature requests.

## Confidentiality and Conflicts of Interest

Reports will be handled with care and shared only with people who need the information to review or address the incident, where reasonably possible.

If a report concerns a maintainer, or a maintainer has a conflict of interest, that person should not participate in deciding the response to the report.

We cannot guarantee absolute confidentiality in every circumstance, but we will make reasonable efforts to protect the privacy of everyone involved.

## Appeals

Anyone subject to an enforcement decision may contact the project maintainers at [phelix@anophel.com](mailto:phelix@anophel.com) to request a review.

Appeals should include relevant context or new information that may help the maintainers reconsider the decision. Appeals will be reviewed where reasonably possible by someone who was not directly involved in the original decision.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), adapted for the Phelix project.

## Updates

The Phelix project maintainers may update this Code of Conduct as the project and community evolve. Continued participation in the community is subject to the version currently published in this repository.

We appreciate everyone who helps make Phelix a respectful, inclusive, and collaborative open-source project.
